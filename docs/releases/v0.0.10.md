## Highlights ⭐️

* **In lockstep with passmcp 0.0.10**: passmcp-reporting is required at v0.0.10, whose attestation verifiers are unchanged from v0.0.9, and the policy rules and hover are cross-checked against passmcp v0.0.10. The language server's own code, its diagnostics and the VS Code extension's behaviour are unchanged from 0.0.9.
* **15-repository family table in the README**: adds the six Apache-2.0 MCP language SDKs (`passmcp-sdk-go`, `passmcp-sdk-python`, `passmcp-sdk-java`, `passmcp-sdk-cpp`, `passmcp-sdk-typescript`, and `passmcp-sdk-rust`) alongside the existing nine family repositories at `0.0.10`.
