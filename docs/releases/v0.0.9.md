## Highlights ⭐️

* **In lockstep with passmcp 0.0.9**: passmcp-reporting is required at v0.0.9, whose attestation verifiers are unchanged from v0.0.8, and the policy rules and hover are cross-checked against passmcp v0.0.9 (hover guidance for new checks such as `protocol.unknown_resource` comes directly from `passmcp explain`). The language server's own code and its diagnostics are unchanged.
* **VS Code extension dependencies and security floors**: `vscode-languageclient` moves to 11.0.0, `@vscode/vsce` to 4.1.0 and `@types/node` to 26.6.6, with `overrides` flooring `@azure/msal-node` at 7.1.0 (GHSA-xcx6-jx8p-x3h5), `minimatch` at `^11.0.1` resolving 11.0.4 (GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249) and `qs` at `^6.16.1` resolving 6.17.1 (GHSA-6pq8-jhm2-2vcw).
* **Manual build lock and workflow pins refreshed**: `certifi` moves to 2026.10.19 in `docs/requirements.txt` (GHSA-6q48-6c7w-9gw2), and GitHub Actions move to `codeql-action` 4.39.3, `sigstore/cosign-installer` 4.1.3, `markdownlint-cli2-action` 25.0.0 and `actions/setup-python` 7.1.0, pinned by commit digest.
